# Louisiana Mortgage & Title Fees (parish-level)  
- **Recording fees (by parish)** – Under La. R.S. 13:844 each clerk follows the statewide tiered schedule. For example, Orleans Parish charges **$100** to record a 1–5 page document, **$200** for 6–25 pages, **$300** for 26–50 pages, plus **$5** for each additional page beyond 50【55†L45-L49】. Jefferson Parish uses a similar bracket schedule (1–5p =$105; 6–25p =$205; 26–50p =$305; +$5 each over 50)【75†L1-L4】. (All parishes index up to 10 names for free; most then charge $5 per extra name【60†L112-L115】【79†L124-L132】.)  
- **Per-page fee** – Covered by the above: after the 50-page threshold, an extra $5/page in Orleans and Jefferson (and similarly in other parishes)【55†L45-L49】【75†L1-L4】. No per-page charge within the stated base ranges.  
- **Indexed name fee** – In Orleans, clerks include up to 10 names in the indexing, then charge **$5 per additional name**【60†L112-L115】. Jefferson likewise charges $5 per name after 10【79†L124-L132】. (By law, maiden/prior names of an indexed person must also be indexed.)  
- **Mortgage certificate fee** – In Orleans, a mortgage certificate (showing existing liens) costs **$20** for the first name and **$10** for each additional name【56†L7-L15】. Jefferson and most parishes use the same $20/$10 structure【79†L131-L134】. (Lien & Privilege certificates are identical fees.) These fees are in addition to recording costs.  
- **Cancellation/release fee** –  Removing a recorded mortgage (“release of mortgage”) generally costs $50–$55. Orleans charges **$50** per instrument (or **$10** if the original note is surrendered)【60†L112-L115】. Jefferson charges **$55** per mortgage cancellation【82†L12-L15】. (This fee is set by statute; some clerks waive most if the original promissory note is presented.)  
- **E-recording surcharge** – All LA parishes participate in the statewide clerks’ portal; a **$5 electronic-recording fee** is collected per document【64†L1-L9】. (The clerk or LCRAA portal generally takes $5; the listing above shows Orleans includes it.)  
- **Local documentary/transfer tax** – Louisiana has *no statewide mortgage or transfer tax* (these lines read *“No”* in Louisiana except New Orleans)【86†L12-L15】. Orleans Parish **does** impose a local documentary transfer tax on mortgages: it is a flat **$325** for mortgages of $9,000 or more (scaled smaller for lower amounts)【67†L51-L59】. Outside New Orleans there is no parish-level transfer or mortgage tax【86†L12-L15】.  

Each of these fields must cite the official source (clerk’s fee schedule or statute). For example, Orleans’s fee sheet documents the base page-fees and indexed-name fees【55†L45-L49】【60†L112-L115】; the Jefferson Clerk confirms its slightly higher brackets and $5 name surcharge【75†L1-L4】【79†L124-L132】.  We would compile similar data for the remaining parishes. (For roadmap parishes beyond Orleans/Jefferson, one would refer to each Parish Clerk’s official “Recording Fees” schedule.) 

# Louisiana Title Insurance Rates & Practices  
- **Lender (Loan) Policy Rates** – Louisiana law fixes title insurance premiums. The state-required lender’s policy schedule (filed with the DOI) charges, for example: **$100 per $1,000** of loan amount up to $12,000; **$4.20 per $1,000** for $12,001–$50,000; **$3.60 per $1,000** for $50,001–$100,000; **$3.30 per $1,000** for $100,001–$500,000; **$2.70 per $1,000** for $500,001–$1,000,000, etc., down to **$1.20 per $1,000** over $35M【45†L808-L817】. Expanded-coverage loan policies (110% coverage) are **10% higher** than these rates【45†L820-L828】.  (All rates must be paid as published; for smaller refinances, the same table applies.)  
- **Refinance/Reissue Credits** – If an owner’s policy already exists, a borrower getting a new owner or loan policy benefits from a **reissue credit**. In Louisiana, the new loan policy premium is **60% of the original full rate** when reissuing off an existing owner’s policy【49†L929-L934】. Similarly, a new owner’s policy on the same land as a prior policy also gets a 60% credit【49†L929-L934】. (Simply refinancing a loan without an existing owner’s policy doesn’t get this credit.)  
- **Common Endorsements** – The LATISSO rate manual includes standard ALTA endorsements. Typical required or requested endorsements on loans include ALTA 9.3 (Covenants/Restrictions), 9.6 (Assessments), 9.6.1 (Current Assessments), 7.1 (Mobile Home), etc. (Coverage forms 9.x and 7.x are adopted by Louisiana, as noted in the manual【42†L43-L52】.) Endorsement rates are also filed and must be charged as listed in the statewide manual.  
- **Closing/Notary Fees** – Louisiana does not regulate title or notary fees for closings. Many loans are closed by notaries (attorneys) with customary closing fees. For example, one New Orleans title company’s published fee sheet shows ~ $350–1,000 for document prep, $250 for title exam, $350 for abstract search, $25 for a closing protection letter, plus recording costs (at actual cost)【51†L67-L75】. Such closing charges vary by company. Importantly, *title insurance premiums* themselves are state-fixed (the same statewide rate)【51†L97-L100】. The clerk data confirm “title insurance premiums are regulated statewide”【51†L97-L100】.  

# Auto Loan Rates & Fees  
- **Interest Rate Benchmarks:** As of 2026, car loan APRs are roughly in the high-single-digits for new cars and low-double-digits for used.  For example, Bankrate reports about **6.97% APR** on a 60-month new-car loan and **7.26% APR** on a 36-month used-car loan (as of May 2026)【89†L223-L227】.  Experian’s Q4 2025 data show national average new-car loan APR ~**6.37%** vs. used-car ~**11.26%**【93†L478-L481】. (Industry surveys also find ~7% for new, ~11% used【94†L61-L64】.) Rates vary by credit tier: prime borrowers (excellent credit) often see rates below these averages, while subprime can be much higher.  For context, the NCUA reported new-car 48‑month APRs around 5.63% at credit unions vs 7.40% at banks (mid-2025)【91†L399-L405】.  
- **Loan Terms:** Typical new/used auto loans run **36–84 months**. Shorter 36–48 month loans usually have lower APR; longer 60–72 month loans rise a bit. (Many lenders offer terms up to 72 or 84 months; e.g. one credit union offers 12–72mo used-car loans【91†L281-L284】, PenFed offers 36–84mo on new cars【91†L258-L261】.) We will collect term-specific benchmarks.  
- **APR vs Nominal:** All quoted rates above are APR (which includes fees)【96†L156-L164】. The nominal interest rate (advertised rate) will be slightly lower than the APR (since APR adds any origination fees or prepaid finance charges【96†L156-L164】). When comparing rates, we ensure to specify APR to account for all costs.  
- **Vehicle/New vs Used:** Used-car loans generally carry higher APR than new. (E.g. 48-month new ~6.83% APR vs 48-month used ~7.40% APR【89†L223-L227】.) We will note assumptions (vehicle age, warranty status) when comparing.  
- **State Title/DMV Fees:** Louisiana’s OMV issues titles for **$68.50** (this covers transfer)【102†L276-L280】. Recording a lien (“record mortgage”) on the title costs **$15** (if done via a UCC financing statement) or **$10** otherwise【102†L276-L280】. A separate $3 “license transfer” fee and $8 handling fee also apply【102†L276-L280】. (No separate notary fee is charged by OMV – notarization of the seller’s signature is required before submitting the title.) These fees are flat, regardless of loan size or LTV.  
- **Auto Transfer/Government Taxes:** Louisiana has an annual vehicle license tax based on value (0.1% of value per year, min $10)【102†L300-L305】, but no special mortgage or transfer tax. (Registration fees or local ad valorem tax on cars are outside scope here.)  
- **Lender/Dealer Fees:** Some lenders charge origination or admin fees (often 1–2% of loan), but many major auto-finance lenders charge *no separate origination fee*, building costs into APR. GAP or extended-warranty financing is optional: if a GAP premium is financed and later canceled, a prorated refund is generally available【103†L39-L47】. Dealers may charge a processing fee (commonly ~$100–300), but these vary and are often rolled into the loan or paid at signing.  
- **Payoff/Prepay:** There is **no federal prepayment penalty** on auto loans; most lenders impose none. A standard payoff quote may have a small $10–$20 processing fee, but again many lenders waive it.  
- **Required vs Optional:** Vehicle title/lien fees and state taxes are **required by law**. Notary fees (for signing) and dealer processing fees are *customary*, not government-fixed. Add-ons (GAP, warranties) and lender convenience fees are *optional or lender-specific*. We will catalog state-legal fees (as above) and typical lender fees from market sources.  

# Personal and Student Loan Fees & Rates  
- **Personal Loans (unsecured):** Origination fees typically **1%–10%** of the loan (often 1–6% for good-credit applicants)【100†L437-L442】; this fee is usually deducted from proceeds (net funded is lower). APRs vary widely by credit: roughly **6%–36% APR** on major platforms【100†L470-L472】. In general, higher-credit borrowers see APRs near the low end; subprime borrowers pay much higher. Most personal loans have **no prepayment penalty**. (For example, SoFi advertises *no origination fee* and *no prepayment penalty*【99†L25-L28】.) We will note the minimum APRs from top lenders and the presence (or absence) of origination fees for personal loans.  
- **Student Loans (Federal vs Private):** For federal loans disbursed in 2024–25, interest rates were ~**6.53%** for undergrad Direct Subsidized/Unsubsidized, **8.08%** for grad Unsubsidized, and **9.08%** for PLUS loans【105†L250-L258】. Origination (disbursement) fees are about **1.057%** (unsubsidized) and **4.228%** (PLUS) of loan amount【105†L271-L280】, taken off the top. For private student loans, advertised APRs range from ~**3.5% to 18%** (typically higher for grad/professional borrowers)【105†L258-L261】. Private student lenders *rarely* charge origination fees (when charged, typically ~1–5%【105†L276-L280】). Crucially, **no prepayment penalties** are allowed on any student loan (federal law prohibits them【105†L304-L306】). Refinancing (consolidation) of student debt: private lenders generally charge **no origination or application fee**【105†L314-L320】, and no prepay penalty.  
- **Credit Tiers:** Rates above assume prime-borrower pricing. Lower-credit borrowers will pay higher APRs. (For example, Experian data show much higher used-car APRs vs. new-car APRs【93†L478-L481】; similarly, credit union vs bank differences【91†L399-L405】.) We will cite available averages: e.g. NCUA and Edmunds figures【91†L399-L405】【94†L61-L64】, and note that individual rates depend on FICO and LTV.
